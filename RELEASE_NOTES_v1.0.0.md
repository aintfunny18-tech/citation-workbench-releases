# Citation Workbench 1.0.0

Citation Workbench checks Bluebook citation form in law-school and practice writing:
memos, briefs, essays, notes, and articles. Choose Bluepages for practitioner documents
or Whitepages for law-review writing, or let Auto decide.

What it checks includes:

- Case names, including Table T6 abbreviations in citations (for example, "Bd. of Educ.").
  Case names in your own sentences are left unabbreviated.
- Reporters, pincites, page ranges, and court-and-date parentheticals, including the
  rule that U.S. Reports citations take only the year.
- Signals ("See, e.g.,", "Cf.") and explanatory parentheticals.
- Statutes, constitutions, and court rules.
- Short forms (Id., supra, short case names) and typeface.

Add source files to compare quotations and find passages that match a pincite. You
decide whether a source supports your argument and whether it is still good law.

Your documents stay on your computer when you use the desktop app. The web edition
processes uploads temporarily and deletes them; results expire after 30 minutes.
